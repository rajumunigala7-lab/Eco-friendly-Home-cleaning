❓ Pricing FAQ

Questions about pricing?

Can I cancel my plan anytime? +

Yes. You can cancel your EcoNest plan whenever you need. Your current service period will remain active until it ends.

Can I change my plan later? +

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan based on your changing needs.

Do yearly plans save money? +

Yes. Choosing yearly billing gives you a 20% discount compared with monthly billing.

Are cleaning products included? +

Yes. Standard cleaning products are included with all EcoNest plans.

Can I request additional services? +

Yes. Additional services may be available depending on your location and requirements. Contact our team for personalized options.
